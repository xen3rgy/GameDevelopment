# Nature material sources

Generated with the built-in Imagegen tool on 2026-10-16 for ZERO // RISE. Reference images informed the art direction; they are not shipped texture pixels. No external model or texture pack was downloaded.

Files: [Leaves.png](Leaves.png), [Bark.png](Bark.png), [Earth.png](Earth.png). The requested 2048-square generations were returned as 1254-square PNGs. Unity imports these at 2048 square with NPOT ToLarger; this resampling is not new photographic detail. Derived height-to-normal and mask maps are 2048-square artistic approximations, not scanned PBR measurements. Original generated PNGs are retained unchanged. `Leaves-Atlas.png` technically repacks the four original alpha silhouettes with transparent gutters into a 2048-square game atlas; aspect ratios and image colors are preserved. This prevents cropped lobes and mip bleeding without inventing new leaf content. Real model detail comes from newly generated branch/leaf geometry and spatial ground meshes.

Tree silhouettes and meshes are authored by WestbruchTreeMeshes.cs. Two geometry LODs, root flare, curved connected branches and small species-matched leaf laminae replace the old imported canopy mesh in the scene. New tree prefabs share these mesh and material assets.

## Final prompts

### tree_leaves

Create a production game texture atlas, square 2048x2048 pixels, TRUE transparent background. Photorealistic botanical color/albedo atlas for modern Unity HDRP European urban trees. A precise 2x2 grid of FOUR isolated SINGLE LEAVES, one centered leaf per quadrant, NO visible grid or labels. TOP LEFT: mature European linden heart-shaped serrated green leaf. TOP RIGHT: birch small triangular serrated green leaf. BOTTOM LEFT: hornbeam elliptical serrated green leaf with fine parallel veins. BOTTOM RIGHT: maple palmate five-lobed green leaf. Each individual leaf points upward, petiole pointing down, blade occupies roughly 80 percent of its square tile height and 70 percent width, keep 8 percent transparent padding inside each tile. Straight overhead orthographic macro botanical photography, diffuse flat illumination, detailed fine veins, organic imperfect edges, tiny subtle insect marks, slight hue variation, matte olive to natural medium green. No cast shadow, no glow, no white border, no background, no stem branches, no cluster of leaves, no perspective, no text. This is an actual alpha-cutout asset to texture small 3D leaf planes, not a presentation sheet or rendered tree. Preserve genuine transparent pixels around and between leaves.

### tree_bark

Create one photorealistic seamless tileable TREE BARK BASE COLOR TEXTURE, square 2048x2048 pixels, for a realistic European urban broadleaf tree in Unity HDRP. Entire image is a close orthographic flat surface of mature gray-brown linden/hornbeam bark, fine irregular vertical shallow fissures, small horizontal age scars, restrained lichen flecks and nuanced neutral weathered brown-grey fibers. Believable actual physical scale: image covers about 0.8 metres width by 1.4 metres height. High-frequency fine detail suitable for real 2K game texture, broad value variation but NO black cavernous cracks, no exaggerated rocks. Flat diffuse overcast color/albedo illumination with no directional lighting, no shiny highlights, no baked hard shadows. Seamlessly repeat left-right and top-bottom. No trunk silhouette, no branches, no leaves, no border, no labels, no text, no object presentation. Fill every pixel with the material.

### urban_earth

Create one photorealistic seamless tileable 2048x2048 pixel BASE COLOR material texture for a realistic worn European low-income apartment courtyard ground, a real game asset for Unity HDRP. Entire square image is flat orthographic downward-facing macro view of compacted grey-brown earth mixed with fine gritty sand, small irregular grey pebbles, occasional dark soil crumbs, tiny dry leaf fragments and fibrous organic matter. Physical coverage about 1.8 by 1.8 metres. Mostly fine earth and sparse varied-size small aggregate, not an all-over bed of bright white gravel. Muted naturally neutral cool tan/grey-brown, damp darker small organic variations, restrained tiny weathering cracks, no massive patterns or perfect circles. Fine photographic 2K detail, diffuse completely flat overcast albedo lighting, no cast shadows or highlights or perspective. Seamless edges horizontally and vertically. No grass clump or large plant, no recognizable objects, no border, no text, no presentation mockup. This will be blended with native sculpted terrain, actual 3D stones and vegetation.
