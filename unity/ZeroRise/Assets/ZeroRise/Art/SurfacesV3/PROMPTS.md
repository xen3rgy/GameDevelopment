# Built-in image_gen prompts

## Material atlas

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: one square 3x3 seamless material atlas for a realistic Windows HDRP European working-class urban game. Generate a 3072x3072 atlas if possible, nine equal square tiles, exact grid edge-to-edge, no margins, frames, labels, text, watermarks, perspective, directional shadows or objects. Orthographic flat scan-like diffuse/albedo surfaces with evenly lit neutral colors. EACH TILE MUST TILE ON ITS OWN; highly detailed small scale natural irregularities, no enormous cartoon cracks or repeated bold blotches. Exact layout row 1 left-to-right: dark cool grey aged asphalt with fine aggregate and fine branching cracks and subtle repairs; worn grey large concrete sidewalk slabs in a 4x4 pattern, narrow irregular dark joints, slight moss, nonwhite; compacted brown-grey dirt and grit with embedded small stones, flattened natural patchiness, NOT uniform golden gravel. Row 2: neglected sparse green-brown grass with visible earth among fine blades, realistic lawn soil surface, muted European greens, NOT lush meadow; light ochre aged European plaster, mostly surviving plaster with small flaked spots, hairline cracks and scattered exposed dark red brick, realistic not extreme all-over peeling; weathered red-brown brick with narrow grey mortar, bricks normal horizontal running bond. Row 3: weathered grey cast concrete with subtle board seams and faint runoff, not repeated dark squares; dry sand with dirt and scattered gravel for a worn playground, subdued beige; mottled dark earth with mud, faded leaf fragments and bare patches for tree beds. These are usable material textures, NOT a rendered environment or reference art guide. Density and realism should evoke the user's old residential courtyards, cracked roads, bus shelters and neglected playground references. Restrained realistic color, no sepia treatment, no flowers, no pasted puddle reflection or baked lighting.

## Transparent foliage atlas

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: ONE transparent game foliage atlas, square 2x2 grid, four equal separate cells, no visible grid lines or labels. Each cell contains a realistic European neglected urban vegetation clump isolated on truly transparent background, entirely inside its cell with 10 percent transparent margin. Front orthographic view with slight natural three-dimensional depth, neutral flat lighting, no cast shadows, ground plane, pots, scenery or baked sky. Top left: asymmetrical low dogwood/bramble shrub with visible slender brown stems and many small realistic green leaves, sparse enough to see stems, no berries. Top right: scruffy nettle and dock weeds, irregular thin stems and green serrated leaves, no ornamental flowers. Bottom left: natural tuft of fine long grass, muted olive green blades with dry straw tips and a few seed heads, realistic grass not triangular spikes. Bottom right: sparse low dry roadside grass and clover weeds, tiny leaves, browned fine blades and irregular silhouette. The four clumps should look distinct and be usable on intersecting mesh cards in a physically lit Unity HDRP game. Photographic texture quality and delicate leaf edges, restrained natural colors, NOT illustrations, rounded balls, stylized shapes, shiny leaves or lush ornamental garden plants. No text, labels, logos, watermarks. Preserve genuine transparent alpha.
