# Westbruch streetscape surface sources

Photographed 2K PBR surface maps from Poly Haven, downloaded 2026-11-02.
All assets are CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license

- Asphalt 02 — https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02
- Pavement 05 — https://polyhaven.com/a/pavement_05
- Brown Mud Leaves 01 — https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud_leaves_01
- Forest Ground 04 — https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_04
- Leafy Grass — https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass

Diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness originals are retained. HDRP mask maps
are technically packed by BahnhofStreetscape from measured roughness; normal
maps are imported as normals, not guessed from diffuse brightness.
The new kerb cross-sections, gutters, covers, tree grilles, border meshes,
planting arrangements and street fixtures are authored in the project.
